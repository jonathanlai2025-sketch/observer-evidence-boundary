# Example: Vendor B

Requested action: pay Vendor B $10,000 for Invoice P-4821.

This example is synthetic. It is not a real transaction, customer, or bank record.

## Agent report

The agent says: “Payment completed.”

Observer: **UNKNOWN**

The actor is reporting on its own action. That is a claim, not independent establishment of the outcome.

## Connector / API

The payment connector or API returns: “Success” / “Request completed.”

This may be recorded as an **execution acknowledgment**: the request was accepted or reported complete by that channel.

It is not independent settlement confirmation.

Observer assessment: still **UNKNOWN**, unless qualifying independent evidence of the intended outcome is present.

Execution acknowledgment is not a fourth assessment state. The assessment axis remains UNKNOWN, CONFIRMED, or DIVERGED.

## Screenshot

A screenshot shows a payment screen.

Observer: **UNKNOWN**

A picture of a record is still a representation. It is not, by itself, independent evidence that Vendor B received the money. A screenshot is not automatically fraud, and it is not automatically proof.

## Ten matching screenshots

Ten similar screenshots, captions, or copies of the same story.

Observer: **UNKNOWN**, unless independence of the sources is separately established.

Repetition is not corroboration. Copies of one representation are still one family of evidence.

## Qualifying independent bank evidence — intended payee

Independent bank evidence shows Vendor B received $10,000 for the intended payment.

Observer: **CONFIRMED**

The claimed outcome is supported by qualifying evidence under the defined rule. This is not a claim of omniscient truth, legal fact, or fraud determination.

## Qualifying independent evidence — wrong payee

Independent bank evidence shows Vendor C received $10,000 instead.

Observer: **DIVERGED**

The evidence conflicts with the claimed outcome.

What the company does next (hold, escalate, investigate, lock a workflow) is a policy response. It is not Observer “deciding to lock,” and it is not cleared by a new receipt, screenshot, retry, or explanation from the agent.
